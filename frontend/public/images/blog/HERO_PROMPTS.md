Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen literary blog background. Create one wide 16:9 editorial landscape photograph of a quiet rugged ocean coast, dark teal water, distant layered cliffs and soft dusk sky. Composition must also work as a center-cropped mobile portrait. No people, buildings, lettering, watermarks or logos.
